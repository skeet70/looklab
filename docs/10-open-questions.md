| Q27 | Qualification and release scope: is a camera's model qualified as a whole, by upload count, or by a scoped check? | **Resolved:** qualification is a scoped claim per model subject and candidate, covering body, epoch, native sim, settings and combinations, output encoding and assessed colour/exposure domain. Preparation availability, numerical validity, publication eligibility, empirical qualification and rooted trust stay separate axes. Promotion, invalidation and evidence requests are automatic through the worker, with no camera-wide badge, count-based promotion or per-camera moderator approval. A source-limited family keeps one identity and refits its existing seed cohort under unchanged gates; another body joins only through scoped admission. A `lut_defined` target uses an immutable `lut_composition` eligibility snapshot over its existing import, frozen approximation and base-pin validity with inherited support and uncertainty instead of a native-family report, with `CurrentTargetEligibility` as the separate derived current status; camera source bases and destination qualification apply normally. A match bundle is an immutable per-`(body, epoch)` revision with its own manifest, published and current-selected independently of target releases; its entries are source refs only, with no status or qualification ref, camera-only updates do not create target releases, and old target-owned bundles stay historical. Qualification dependencies are scoped by output path: settings need the destination realization and control-response model, a card needs a valid target and card, and a software export needs its insertion-point dependencies. A broad experimental release with the reference body's working baseline is distinct from a cross-body-qualified shared-family launch. | 03 §1, 04 §1, 05 §8, 07 §5, 08 §0.3, 09 |
| Q28 | Does a camera carry an absolute scene-XYZ calibration claim? | **Resolved:** no. Native prediction is a conditional mean-rendering claim under pinned calibration, documented held-out nuisance treatment and an assessed domain. Calibration source and confidence are dependencies and limitations, not an absolute-accuracy badge, and no per-camera absolute calibration evidence is assumed. | 02 §5, 04 §1, 08 §2.5 |
